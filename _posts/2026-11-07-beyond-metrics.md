Hi, thank you for your interest in this project!

We trained five procedural personas for a continuous game arena and investigated whether humans could tell them apart perceptually. The result is our AIIDE 2026 paper, Beyond Metrics, and this blog post is a hub for its main resources.

If you want to our paper, you can go to this link: [Beyond Metrics: A Human Validation Study of Procedural Personas in a Continuous Game Arena](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIIDE/article/view/43598).

If you're interested in watching my presentation explaining this paper in 5 minutes you can [watch this YouTube video](https://youtu.be/DKtCmnKeZnw).

In case you wanna check the videos of the agents playing by yourself, head to our [Zenodo repository](https://zenodo.org/records/22959121).

If you're interested in checking the source code for our implementation (or in case you want to try the personas by yourself), check out the paper's [GitHub release](https://github.com/Re-G3X/ml-agents/releases/tag/paper-aiide-2026).

Have I mentioned that this project started when we found a gap in the literature? If you'd like to read the full paper yourself, check out this [systematic mapping study](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11755901).

I hope this research contributes to your knowledge. Please, feel free to check my social media at this website, and send me a message on LinkedIn if you're interested in collaboration.

Many thanks, and see you soon!:)
