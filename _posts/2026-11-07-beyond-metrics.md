Hi, thank you for your interest in this project!

We trained five procedural personas for a continuous game arena and investigated whether humans could tell them apart perceptually. The result is our AIIDE 2026 paper, Beyond Metrics, and this short post is a hub for its main resources.

If you want to our paper, you can go to this link: [Beyond Metrics: A Human Validation Study of Procedural Personas in a Continuous Game Arena](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIIDE/article/view/43598).

If you want to see the videos of the agents playing and check the questionnaire's dataset, head to our [Zenodo repository](https://zenodo.org/records/22959121).

If you're interested in checking the source code for our implementation (or in case you want to try the personas by yourself), check out the paper's [GitHub release](https://github.com/Re-G3X/ml-agents/releases/tag/paper-aiide-2026).

If you're interested in deepening your knowledge about persona-based agents, I'd like to recommend our [systematic mapping study](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11755901), that directly inspired this paper.

And, if you understand Portuguese, there is a simplified version of this mapping which was presented as a concise literature review at São Paulo State University's WPPGCC event. You can check out my blog post about this presentation [here](https://varalta.github.io/blog/2026/wppgcc-2025/).

I hope our research contributes to your knowledge. Please, feel free to check my social media at this website, and send me a message on LinkedIn if you're interested in collaborations.

Thank you, see you soon!
